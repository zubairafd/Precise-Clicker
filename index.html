FAQ

Questions, answered

Does the app read my screen or collect data?

No. The Tap service only performs one tap at the point you choose. The app contains no ads and collects no personal data. It uses the internet only to check the time.

Why does it need the Accessibility service?

Android only allows an app to perform taps for you through the Accessibility service. It is used solely for the single scheduled tap.

How accurate is it?

Timing is usually within a few milliseconds of the target. Real-world results also depend on network jitter and your device, so the app includes a lead-time setting and a test tap to fine-tune it.

Will it work in every game or app?

Most apps accept it, but some games and secure screens block automated taps. Always try the Test tap button first.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free.
